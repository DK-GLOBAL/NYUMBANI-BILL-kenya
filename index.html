Who we serve

Landlords and property managers who want rent collected by M-Pesa with automatic receipts and arrears tracking, and tenants looking for verified vacant homes anywhere in Kenya.

Rent payments are processed by Safaricom M-Pesa. We never see or store your M-Pesa PIN.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

Landlords pay KSh 150 per occupied tenant per month (slightly negotiable). House-seekers pay a one-time referral fee only if we place them: KSh 500 (bedsitter) or KSh 1,000 (larger units).

How does a landlord get a login?

Landlord accounts are created by our Platform Admin once onboarding is complete. Landlords then add their tenants and can add assistants with limited access.

What happens if monthly fees are not paid?

The Platform Admin may suspend the account until the unpaid monthly fees are settled.

How do tenants pay rent?

By M-Pesa STK push: you get a prompt on your phone, enter your PIN and a receipt is issued.

Why is my unit not listed yet?

Every unit is checked and approved by our team before it appears under Find a Home.

How do I get help?

Call or WhatsApp 0780 490 437, or email info@nyumbanibill.com.

Terms and privacy (summary)

Terms. Use Nyumbani Bill honestly: list only units you are authorised to let, and keep your login private. We may remove listings or accounts that mislead tenants or break the law. Fees are as shown on the Pricing tab and in your agreement. Accounts with unpaid monthly fees may be suspended by the Platform Admin.

Privacy. We collect your name, phone, email and tenancy and payment details to run the service. We handle them in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, share them only with the parties to your tenancy and payment providers, and never sell them. To access, correct or delete your data, email info@nyumbanibill.com.
